# FS-005 — Normative Requirements

Evaluation classifications:

- **M** — mechanical
- **S** — semantic
- **B** — both mechanical and semantic

## Requirements

### FS-005-NR-001 — Exact Design Binding
**Classification: M**
The FS-005 scope and Plan shall identify the exact consumed Product Design revision.

### FS-005-NR-002 — External Governed Reconstruction
**Classification: B**
Before ordinary author-workshop operations, the Product shall reconstruct applicable accepted and candidate governed state from the external persisted Dataset and compatible Ruleset without relying on prior conversation or private model memory.

### FS-005-NR-003 — Compatibility Gate
**Classification: B**
Ordinary FS-005 authoring and persistence operations shall require compatibility established under FS-002.

### FS-005-NR-004 — Derived Author Views
**Classification: B**
The Product shall provide derived views for current story, Canon, Plot, Prose, Manuscript, candidate, and bounded readiness state without making those views story authority.

### FS-005-NR-005 — Conversation Is Not Acceptance
**Classification: S**
Conversation, model suggestion, summary, critique, or generated view shall not by itself create accepted story meaning, production approval, Manuscript acceptance, or persistence authorization.

### FS-005-NR-006 — New Canon Candidate
**Classification: B**
The Product shall permit new identifiable Canon Character, Setting, or Event candidates with candidate semantic authority and durable revision without altering accepted Canon merely because the proposal exists.

### FS-005-NR-007 — New Plot Candidate
**Classification: B**
The Product shall permit new synopsis, outline, or sequence Plot candidates with candidate semantic authority and durable revision while preserving Canon/Plot ownership.

### FS-005-NR-008 — Pending Candidate Durability
**Classification: B**
A pending semantic or production candidate may be persisted and reconstructed without becoming accepted or approved merely because it was saved.

### FS-005-NR-009 — Candidate Refinement
**Classification: B**
A pending new-artifact candidate shall be revisable while preserving stable identity, retaining prior candidate revision provenance, and assigning a distinct current candidate revision.

### FS-005-NR-010 — Explicit Semantic Acceptance
**Classification: B**
A new Canon or Plot candidate shall become accepted semantic state only through an explicit acceptance operation at its identifiable owning scope.

### FS-005-NR-011 — Material Dependency Closure
**Classification: B**
Acceptance or production approval shall refuse a candidate whose material dependency is missing, unresolved, stale, or remains candidate outside the accepted scope.

### FS-005-NR-012 — Coordinated Acceptance
**Classification: B**
The Product shall permit explicitly scoped coordinated acceptance when every material candidate dependency is already accepted or explicitly included in the same coordinated scope; scope shall not expand silently.

### FS-005-NR-013 — New Prose/Production Candidate
**Classification: B**
The Product shall permit Beats, Modes, and pseudo-prose to be created as production candidates without granting production approval.

### FS-005-NR-014 — Explicit Production Approval
**Classification: B**
A production candidate shall govern ordinary generation only after explicit production approval and applicable dependency closure.

### FS-005-NR-015 — Progressive Refinement
**Classification: B**
The workflow shall support progressive development from governed Canon and Plot through local Prose controls to generation readiness without requiring fixed whole-story development order.

### FS-005-NR-016 — Bounded Generation Readiness
**Classification: B**
For a supplied scene scope, the Product shall determine whether current governed state is sufficient for ordinary generation and, when ready, allow existing FS-003 context, contract, and package construction.

### FS-005-NR-017 — Safe Not-ready State
**Classification: B**
When required consequential meaning, accepted dependency, production approval, information boundary, stopping boundary, or reconciliation state is insufficient, readiness shall remain false/not-ready rather than inventing missing meaning.

### FS-005-NR-018 — View Freshness and Authority Separation
**Classification: B**
Author views shall be generated from current governed state and reflect current candidate/accepted status sufficiently that rendered summary text is not treated as acceptance evidence.

### FS-005-NR-019 — Accepted Upstream Revision Integration
**Classification: B**
Revision of already-accepted Canon or Plot through the author-workshop facade shall preserve FS-004 revision and reconciliation semantics.

### FS-005-NR-020 — FS-003 Production Handoff
**Classification: B**
Once a scene is generation-ready, the author-workshop runtime shall use the current FS-003 context/contract/package path without manual storage translation.

### FS-005-NR-021 — Candidate Withdrawal
**Classification: B**
The author shall be able to withdraw a pending new-artifact candidate without altering accepted sibling or upstream state, and withdrawn state shall not continue to govern readiness.

### FS-005-NR-022 — Coherent Persistence
**Classification: B**
An authorized FS-005 save shall persist one coherent Dataset state containing applicable accepted and pending candidate authoring state or fail without reporting partial success.

### FS-005-NR-023 — Persistence Conflict Safety
**Classification: M**
A stale FS-005 session shall not silently overwrite newer persisted Dataset state.

### FS-005-NR-024 — Fresh-session Reconstruction
**Classification: B**
After persistence, a fresh runtime shall reconstruct accepted and pending candidate state, production approvals, revision/reconciliation information, and bounded readiness sufficiently to continue authoring.

### FS-005-NR-025 — No Silent Consequential Defaults
**Classification: S**
The Product shall not fill missing consequential Canon, Plot, Prose intent, reveal timing, or Manuscript wording merely because a complete value is easier to represent.

### FS-005-NR-026 — End-to-end Author-workshop Demonstration
**Classification: S**
Semantic Review shall demonstrate an author progressing an incomplete external Dataset through Canon, Plot, Prose refinement, readiness, FS-003 preparation, persistence/reconstruction, and an accepted-upstream FS-004 revision without manual Dataset editing as the author interaction model.

### FS-005-NR-027 — Requirement Evaluation Bindings
**Classification: M**
Every active M requirement and mechanically decidable portion of each active B requirement shall have at least one exact Product Validation-task binding, and semantic-only requirements shall not be mechanically claimed.

### FS-005-NR-028 — Dataset Boundary
**Classification: B**
Story-instance Dataset state and any external reference fixture shall remain outside this Ruleset repository.
