# FS-008 — Normative Requirements

Evaluation classifications:

- **M** — mechanical
- **S** — semantic
- **B** — both mechanical and semantic

### FS-008-NR-001 — Exact Design Binding
**Classification: M**

FS-008 Planning shall identify the exact accepted Product Design revision that
defines DP-200 live dependency and semantic-alignment semantics.

### FS-008-NR-002 — Stable Live Dependency Identity
**Classification: B**

A live governed dependency on a continuing artifact shall identify that artifact
by stable identity and shall not use a historical content revision as the
semantic identity of the relationship.

### FS-008-NR-003 — Current Accepted Resolution
**Classification: B**

Readiness, compilation, and current-state evaluation shall resolve a live
dependency against the target artifact's current accepted governed state.

### FS-008-NR-004 — Revision-specific Alignment Evidence
**Classification: B**

The Product shall preserve revision-specific evidence of the upstream revisions
against which a dependent scope was last aligned, separately from the live
dependency relation.

### FS-008-NR-005 — Upstream Revision Impact
**Classification: B**

A material accepted revision of a continuing upstream artifact shall identify
affected dependents by stable target identity and shall make non-current
alignment reviewable without replacing that identity.

### FS-008-NR-006 — Reconciliation Preserves Continuing Identity
**Classification: B**

Successful reconciliation against a continuing upstream artifact shall advance
alignment evidence to the reviewed revision while preserving the live target
identity.

### FS-008-NR-007 — Frozen Generation Provenance
**Classification: M**

A used generation package shall retain the exact selected revisions that
governed that attempt, and later accepted upstream revisions shall not mutate
that frozen provenance.

### FS-008-NR-008 — Earlier Manuscript Independence
**Classification: B**

A scene shall be capable of becoming generation-ready without earlier scene
Manuscript prose when its required accepted Canon, Plot, Prose, viewpoint, and
other governed semantic state is available.

### FS-008-NR-009 — No Routine Prior-Manuscript Accumulation
**Classification: M**

Routine scene context and generation-package construction shall not
automatically accumulate accepted Manuscript preceding the target scene by
ordinal.

### FS-008-NR-010 — Bounded Scene-generation Scale
**Classification: B**

Routine generator-facing context shall scale with the target scene's material
governed needs rather than with the count or size of preceding Manuscript
artifacts.

### FS-008-NR-011 — Governed Legacy Dependency Migration
**Classification: B**

A compatible Dataset containing legacy revision-pinned live dependency
relations shall require an explicit governed migration that moves the historical
revision basis into alignment evidence and removes the revision pin from the
live relation.

### FS-008-NR-012 — Historical Provenance Preservation
**Classification: M**

Dependency-representation migration shall not rewrite frozen generation
packages, generation provenance, revision history, or reconciliation history.

### FS-008-NR-013 — Chained Compatibility Transitions
**Classification: M**

When an authorized Dataset requires more than one known compatibility
transition before ordinary work is directly compatible, session initialization
shall apply the known transitions in order and fail safe if compatibility still
cannot be established.

### FS-008-NR-014 — Canon Continuity Authority
**Classification: S**

Consequential cross-scene facts, states, and occurrences required by later
governed reasoning shall be represented at their owning Canon or Plot semantic
surface rather than remaining authoritative only in Manuscript prose.

### FS-008-NR-015 — Consequential Invention Escalation
**Classification: S**

Consequential meaning discovered in downstream prose that is absent from its
owning semantic surface shall be surfaced for upstream author resolution and
shall not silently become Canon or Plot authority.

### FS-008-NR-016 — Scene Semantic Closure
**Classification: S**

A scene is semantically closed only when its applicable Plot, Prose,
Manuscript, and consequential Canon effects are aligned against current
accepted upstream semantic authority.

### FS-008-NR-017 — Requirement Evaluation Bindings
**Classification: M**

Every active mechanical requirement and mechanically decidable portion of every
active mixed requirement in FS-008 shall have an exact Product Validation task
binding, and semantic-only requirements shall not be mechanically claimed.

### FS-008-NR-018 — Dataset Boundary
**Classification: M**

Reference story-instance Dataset state used for FS-008 validation shall remain
outside the Ruleset repository except for bounded synthetic Product Validation
fixtures.
