# Prose, Style, Voice, and Manuscript

## Purpose

Prose realizes accepted intended occurrence into readable fiction while
preserving accepted Canon and Plot meaning.

Manuscript stores author-facing accepted final text.

## Governed Prose Workflow

Prose development may refine through:

- Beats,
- Modes,
- Pseudo-prose,
- Final prose.

Final prose is manuscript output, not a fourth persisted governed Prose
workflow component.

## Beats

Beats are the smallest governed narrative-intent units used to prepare accepted
Sequence meaning for prose realization.

They should be detailed enough to guide realization without unnecessarily
turning prose into rigid screenplay-like structure.

## Modes

Narrative modes include at least:

- action,
- dialogue,
- exposition,
- internal experience.

Modes help develop and review prose realization while remaining
machine-interpretable.

Additional modes may be introduced only when Product Design establishes a
meaningful need.

## Pseudo-Prose

Pseudo-prose is near-prose realization that establishes order,
paragraph-scale flow, emphasis, transitions, viewpoint handling, and rough
wording without requiring final literary polish.

Pseudo-prose remains governed working material rather than accepted manuscript
unless the author explicitly accepts equivalent wording as manuscript.

## Non-Consequential Detail

Prose may add sensory, expressive, connective, rhythmic, or descriptive detail
needed for readable fiction when that detail does not materially constrain
later story reasoning.

Consequential additions follow DP-110 and remain proposals until accepted at
their owning Canon or Plot layer.

## Persistent Prose Guidance

Long-form fiction often requires persistent realization policy independent of
individual Beats.

The product may therefore maintain accepted prose guidance covering matters
such as:

- narrative voice,
- viewpoint policy,
- tense,
- register,
- stylistic tendencies,
- dialogue conventions,
- genre realization constraints,
- lexical or period constraints,
- and other persistent authorial preferences.

Persistent prose guidance governs realization but does not become Canon truth
about the fictional world.

The exact storage model and whether guidance is global, scoped, or layered are
Planning decisions.

## Viewpoint

Viewpoint is semantically important prose guidance.

The product must be able to distinguish intended viewpoint policy from
incidental model behavior.

Viewpoint may vary by manuscript unit where the author intends it.

## Manuscript

Manuscript is author-facing accepted final prose.

Generated candidate prose does not become manuscript merely because it was
produced.

Accepted manuscript text may be assembled, replaced, revised, reordered, or
retitled only through author-governed operations.

## Manuscript Mapping

A manuscript unit may realize one or more accepted Plot or Prose regions.

Stable manuscript identity is independent of filename, title, and ordinal.

The persisted manuscript manifest or equivalent structure must support durable
order and targeting.

## Manuscript and Upstream Meaning

Accepting manuscript text does not silently resolve consequential upstream
proposals.

When accepted manuscript depends on consequential new meaning, that meaning
must be accepted at its owning layer before or as part of manuscript
acceptance.

If upstream accepted meaning later changes, affected manuscript becomes subject
to DP-120 reconciliation.
